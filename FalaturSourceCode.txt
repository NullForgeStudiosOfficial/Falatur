**Falatur** _(fal- + -tur)_ is a Quenya-inspired name chosen to represent the ideals of the system rather than a direct translation.<br><br>

Drawing from Quenya roots associated with **foam** (_fal-_) and **mastery** (_-tur_), the name symbolizes the pursuit of excellence through the practice of foam combat.<br><br>

Falatur is founded on the belief that combat is not merely about victory, but about honor, discipline, craftsmanship, and continual self-improvement.

# The Rule Of Honor

Falatur is built upon trust and honor.

The purpose of the Rule of Honor is not to prevent cheating. It is to ensure that winning is never more important than integrity.


Falatur depends more upon its players than its rules. Every player is both a competitor and an official. Without trust, no honor-based combat system can function. <br>
<div class="mylinebreak"></div>
<br>



<div class='descriptive'>
Every opponent trusts you with their safety. Honor that trust.
</div>



Players are expected to acknowledge valid hits, respect their opponents, and uphold both the written rules and the spirit in which they were intended.

No ruleset can account for every situation. When uncertainty arises, players are expected to resolve it honestly rather than seek advantage through technicalities.

<br><br>
<div class='descriptive'>
Winning through dishonesty is not a victory.
</div>

If two players genuinely disagree, neither should assume the other is acting dishonestly. 

Resolve the disagreement respectfully or request a Judge.

<br><br>
<div class='descriptive'>
When in doubt, favor your opponent.
</div>

# Weapons

Weapons are the primary means of combat in Falatur.

Every weapon must:<div class="mylinebreak"></div>

- Pass inspection.
- Be assigned a single damage type.
- Be marked with the appropriate color band.

A weapon may only be assigned a single Primary Damage Type during inspection. Additional damage types granted by weapon properties, abilities, or other bonuses do not change the weapon's Primary Damage Type.
<br>
Weapons capable of multiple methods of attack must declare a single damage type during inspection.
<br>
Once approved, a weapon's damage type may not be changed without being reinspected by a Judge.<br>
Judges are to use their best discretion when color banding a weapon.<br> (e.g. not assigning a mace the slashing damage type)<br> <br>

While many historical weapons were capable of striking in several different ways, Weapons are limited to a single damage type to promote clear gameplay, meaningful weapon choice, and consistent judging.<br>

<br>

## Weapon Damage Types

Weapons are assigned a color band according to their damage type.
<br> <br>

There are three damage types. 
- **Piercing:** Indicated by the color <span style="color:#009900;">**Green**</span>
- **Slashing:** Indicated by the color <span style="color:#ff0000;">**Red**</span>
- **Bludgeoning:** Indicated by the color <span style="color:#0000ff;">**Blue**</span>





\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

### Piercing Damage
Piercing weapons concentrate force into a single point, slipping past armor and striking the target beneath.<br>

• Ignores Armor equal to its Piercing damage.<br>
• Deals No damage to Shields<br>
• Piercing damage cannot Sever or Break limbs. Damage dealt to a limb instead reduces that limb's Hit Points normally.<br> <br>



### Slashing Damage
• Regular damage to armor.<br>
• Deals No damage to Shields.<br>
• Slashing damage removes limbs.<br>
&ensp; • If an arm is slashed: If the arm was holding a weapon,shield, or item. It is to be dropped immediantly. (those with strap shields may simply let the arm fall, as unstrapping a shield mid-combat usually results in death.)<br>
&ensp; • If a leg is slashed: The user most remove the leg from the ground, and balance on the undamaged leg, hopping if necessary, or kneeling if they prefer.<br>

&ensp; If a severed limb is struck again, it is treated as though it is no longer present. The attack continues naturally into any valid target behind it.
<br> <br>


### Bludgeoning Damage
• Double damage to armor.<br>
• Deals damage to Shields.<br>
• Bludgeoning damage breaks limbs.<br>
&ensp; • If an arm is broken: It must fall limp upon being hit, the damaged arm cannot be used.<br>
&ensp;• If a leg is broken: The user most not use the leg to move, and balance on the undamaged leg, hopping if necessary, or kneeling if they prefer.<br>
&ensp; If a broken limb is hit again by bludgeoning damage, no effect occures, and the player does not lose anymore hit points. 
<br> <br>



\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

#### Combined Damage
If a limb is reduced to 0 Hit Points by multiple damage types, the injury suffered is determined by the damage type that reduced the limb to 0 Hit Points.<br><br>

If an attack deals more than one damage type, only the weapon's primary damage type determines injuries to limbs. Any secondary damage types affect Armor, Shields, or other applicable game mechanics normally.
<br> <br>

## Weapon Lengths
The length of a weapon determines its "size." Falatur weapons come in three sizes. <br><br>

**Small:** The weapon must be no longer than the distance from your hip to your knee. <br>
If the weapon exceeds this length, it is considered a Medium weapon.<br><br>

**Medium:** The weapon must be longer than the distance from your hip to your knee, but shorter than the distance from your hip to your foot. <br>
If it is longer, it is considered an illegal weapon. <br> 
<br>

**Large:** The weapon must be longer than the distance from your foot to your shoulder.

## Weapon Families
Weapons are constructed in many different ways. Certain weapon families gain additional properties based on their design.<br><br>

While many weapons share similar dimensions, they often perform very differently in combat. Weapon families allow these differences to be represented consistently without relying on numerous construction exceptions or special-case rules. <br><br>

### Daggers
A Dagger is a Small weapon with a continuous rigid blade.<br>
Daggers deal **One(1)**  Piercing damage.
<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Slashing

When making a slashing attack, a Dagger deals **One(1)** Slashing damage instead of its normal damage type.
</div>

### Rapiers
A Rapier is a Medium weapon with a continuous rigid blade terminating in a distinct thrusting tip.A Rapier must also possess an enclosed hand guard, such as a basket hilt, for easy identification.<br>
The hand guard exists solely for weapon identification and offers no protection to the wielder's hand.
<br>
Rapiers deal **Two(2)** Piercing damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Slashing
When making a slashing attack, a Rapier deals **One(1)**  Slashing damage instead of its normal damage type.
</div>

### Spears

A Spear is a Large weapon consisting of a rigid shaft that terminates in a distinct thrusting tip. <br>

Spears deal **Two(3)** Piercing damage.<br> 

When wielded with only one hand, Spears deal **Two(2)** Piercing damage.<br> <br>


\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>



### Swords

A Sword is a weapon with a continuous rigid blade and an exposed handle no longer than one-third of its overall length. <br>

Swords deal **Two(2)** Slashing Damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Momentum

If the Sword is considered a **Large** weapon, it also deals **One(1)** Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
</div>

### Axes

An Axe is a weapon with a rigid axe head mounted to a haft. The axe head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. <br>

Axes deal **Two(2)** Slashing Damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Momentum

If the Axe is considered a **Large** weapon, it also deals **One(1)** Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
</div>

### Clubs

A Club is a weapon with a continuous rigid striking surface and no defined edge. The exposed handle must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. <br>

Clubs deal **Two(2)** Bludgeoning Damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Momentum

If the Club is considered a **Large** weapon, it deals **Four(4)** Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
</div>

### Hammers

A Hammer is a weapon with a distinct rigid striking head mounted to a haft. The striking head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length. <br>

Hammers deal **Two(2)** Bludgeoning Damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Momentum

If the Hammer is considered a **Large** weapon, it deals **Four(4)** Bludgeoning Damage when wielded with two hands.
</div>


\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>





### Flails

A Flail is a weapon with a distinct striking head connected to a haft by a flexible tether.<br>

A Flail must extend above the wielder's elbow, but must not reach the ground when measured from the wielder's elbow. The striking head must not exceed one-third of the weapon's overall length.<br>

Flails deal **Two(2)** Bludgeoning damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: None

Flails have no additional properties.
</div>


### Bows

A Bow is a ranged weapon designed to launch arrows. Bows may not be used to make melee attacks.<br>

A Bow must not exceed a draw weight of 30 pounds at a 30-inch draw.<br>

An arrow deals 1 Piercing damage when used as a thrusting weapon.<br>
An arrow deals 4 Piercing damage when fired from a Bow.<br>
An arrow must weigh less than 16 oz.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Ammunition

A Bow requires an arrow to be nocked before it may be fired.
</div>

### Crossbows

A Crossbow is a ranged weapon designed to launch bolts. Crossbows may not be used to make melee attacks.
<br>
A Crossbow must not exceed a draw weight of 75 pounds.
<br>
A bolt deals 1 Piercing damage when used as a thrusting weapon.<br>
A bolt deals 2 Piercing damage when fired from a Crossbow.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Ammunition

A Crossbow requires a bolt to be nocked before it may be fired.
</div>

\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>



### Halberds

A Halberd is a polearm with multiple functional striking surfaces mounted to a rigid haft. When the butt of the haft rests on the ground beside the wielder, the highest striking surface must reach at least the wielder's shoulder.<br>

Each striking surface must be clearly distinguishable. <br>

A Halberd must possess at least two distinct functional striking surfaces of different weapon types. <br>

Halberds deal 1 damage of the appropriate damage type for the striking surface used.<br>

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Versatile

A Halberd may deal Slashing, Piercing, or Bludgeoning damage depending on the striking surface used.
</div>


### Javelins

A Javelin is a ranged weapon with a rigid shaft terminating in a distinct thrusting tip. <br>

A Javelin must weigh less than 16 ounces.<br>

Javelins deal 2 Piercing damage when thrown.<br>
Javelins deal 1 Piercing damage when used as a thrusting weapon.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: Thrown

A Javelin must be thrown to deal its full damage.
</div>

### Whips

A Whip is a flexible weapon consisting of a handle connected to a flexible striking length.<br>

Whips must be in 6 inch sections, with at most a 1 inch gap between them.  <br>

Whips deal **Two(2)** Bludgeoning damage.

<div class='descriptive'>
#### Property: None

Whips have no additional properties.
</div>

## Weapon Construction 
All weapons, shields, and equipment used in Falatur must be constructed with player safety as the highest priority.<br>

A Judge may reject any equipment they believe presents an unreasonable risk of injury, regardless of whether it technically complies with these rules.<br><br>

## Inspection

All equipment must pass inspection before being used in combat.<br>

Equipment that becomes damaged or unsafe during play must be removed from the battlefield immediately.
<br><br>
## General Requirements
Weapons must:

- Be constructed to safely simulate the weapon they represent.
- Be free of exposed cores, sharp edges, or hard protrusions.
- Have all striking surfaces completely covered in cloth.
- Be securely assembled with no loose or shifting components.
- Remain safe when used at full combat force.

<br>

## Striking Surfaces

Every striking surface must be adequately padded.<br>

No striking surface may pass more than **0.5 inches** through a **2.5-inch diameter** opening when tested in the direction of the strike.<br><br>

\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

## Non-Striking Surfaces

Crossguards, pommels, basket hilts, and other non-striking components must be padded.<br>

No non-striking component may pass more than **0.5 inches** through a **2-inch diameter** opening.<br><br>

## Coverings
All striking surfaces must be completely covered in cloth. <br><br>

The padding of a striking surface must remain capable of compressing freely. No covering or reinforcement may significantly reduce the effectiveness of the padding.<br>

Tape, paint, coatings, or any other material that significantly alters the softness or impact characteristics of a striking surface are prohibited.<br><br>

### Padding

The flat of every bladed weapon must be safely padded.<br>

Hafts on swung weapons must be safely padded, excluding a reasonable handhold.<br><br>

## Flex

Weapons must not flex excessively during normal use.<br>

A Judge may reject any weapon they believe flexes beyond safe limits.<br><br>





\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>


## Cores

Weapon cores must never become exposed during normal use.<br>

Metal cores are prohibited, except where explicitly permitted for approved projectile construction.<br><br>

### Core Security
A weapon's core must not rotate, shift, or otherwise move independently of the weapon.<br><br>

The end of every rigid core must terminate in a securely attached retention plate or equivalent structure.

The retention plate must be at least twice the diameter of the core and be capable of preventing the core from penetrating the weapon's padding if the surrounding adhesive or foam fails.<br><br>

#### Approved Retention Methods
Every rigid core must have a securely attached retention plate or equivalent structure at each end.<br><br>

The retention plate must have a diameter at least twice that of the core and be sufficient to prevent the core from advancing through the weapon if the surrounding foam or adhesive fails.<br> <br>

This requirement applies to all fired projectiles, weapon tips, and pommels.<br><br>

<div class='descriptive'>
There are no strict requirements for the material used as a retention plate. Pennies, washers, and countless other materials have all been used successfully.<br><br>
  Use what you have available. Just make sure it is securely attached and capable of preventing the core from advancing if the surrounding foam or adhesive fails.
</div>

# Hit Points
All players have the same number of Hit Points.<br>

Each arm, each leg, and the torso have **2 Hit Points**.<br><br>

A player is killed when their torso is reduced to **0 Hit Points**, regardless of the condition of their limbs.<br>
A killed player must be Revived before returning to play in a skirmish.<br><br>

If a limb at 0 Hit Points is struck again, the damage is transferred to the torso instead, unless the limb is Severed.
<br><br>

## Hit Locations

The following are considered part of the Arm:<br>
• Hand<br>
• Forearm<br>
• Upper arm<br>
• Shoulder<br><br>

The following are considered part of the Leg:<br>
• Foot<br>
• Lower Leg<br>
• Thigh<br><br>


The following are considered part of the Torso:<br>
• Chest<br>
• Abdomen<br>
• Back<br>
• Hips<br>
• Underarm<br><br>

<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>



## Valid Hits
A slashing weapon may perform a **draw cut** by sliding its striking surface across a target with reasonable force.<br>
If a player catches or grabs the striking surface of a slashing weapon, the hand is considered hit and the weapon must immediately be released.<br><br>

A piercing attack may be delivered by either a thrust or a push. A push is the application of continuous forward pressure with a legal striking tip until both players acknowledge that sufficient force has been applied under the Rule Of Honor.<br><br>

A hit is only valid if both players acknowledge that it was delivered with reasonable force, as described in the Rule Of Honor, and it is not considered an Illegal Hit.

<br><br>

## Illegal Hits
A hit to a player's head or groin results in the attacker's immediate death, regardless of armor or remaining Hit Points.<br>
<div class='descriptive'>
If the struck player believes the hit was accidental, they may waive this penalty under the Rule Of Honor
</div>
	

No portion of a weapon may be used to protect a valid target.<br>  Any body part enclosed by a weapon remains a valid target. *(e.g. a basket hilt on a rapier.)* <br>
<div class='descriptive'>
Shields are exempt from this rule.
</div>
	

A player may not use a weapon to cover a valid target in an attempt to prevent damage.<br> If a weapon is positioned over a valid target, the covered body part is still considered struck.<br> *(e.g. laying the flat of a sword across the torso.)*
<div class='descriptive'>
Shields are exempt from this rule.
</div>

A weapon must strike a player directly to score a valid hit.<br> Hits resulting solely from a weapon rebounding or deflecting off another weapon do not count.

## Limb States
A limb can be in one of three states.<br>
<br>

### Healthy
The limb is undamaged, and functions normally. <br>
Limbs are restored to the Healthy state upon the player being Revived.<br><br>

### Severed
Limbs are Severed by weapons dealing Slashing Damage. <br>
A severed limb may no longer be used.<br><br>

If the limb was an arm:<br> If it was holding anything, it is immediately dropped. 
<div class='descriptive'>
Those using strap shields may simply allow the arm to hang, as attempting to remove the shield during combat may result in death.
</div>

If the limb was a leg:<br> The player must begin balancing on the other leg, hopping if necessary.<br>
A second successful hit to a severed limb results in the player's death.
<br> <br>

### Broken
Limbs are broken by weapons dealing Bludgeoning Damage. <br>

A broken limb may hold a weapon, shield, or item, but may not attack, block, or perform any action requiring normal use of that limb.<br><br>

Weapons or shields held by a broken limb may still receive incoming strikes normally.<br> <br>

\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

## Death
A player is considered dead when their torso is reduced to **0 Hit Points**, or when two limbs have been severed.<br><br>

A dead player must immediately lie on the ground at the location of their death and remain there until Revived.<br>
If playing a respawn match, the player must instead return to their designated respawn point.
<div class='descriptive'>
A player may slightly adjust their position for comfort, but may not obstruct living players.

Dead players may call for a Medic, but may not otherwise communicate or participate in the battle.
</div>

## Reviving
Reviving a player restores all Hit Points and fully repairs their Armor.<br><br>

Any player may revive their own revivable items.<br><br>

To revive a player or item, maintain uninterrupted physical contact with both hands while audibly counting **"Revivify One"** through **"Revivify Ten."**<br>
If the countdown is interrupted, or you are struck, the revivification fails and must be restarted.<br><br>

### Medics
Only designated Medics may revive players.<br><br>

To qualify as a Medic:<br>

- You may not wear armor.
- You must be approved by a Judge.
- You must wear a clearly visible Medic insignia.

A Medic automatically revives 30 seconds after death.

# Armor
As there are multiple different types of armor in the world<br>

We will simplify them down to three types: Light, Medium, and Heavy<br><br>

Armor classifications are determined by the weight of each individual armor piece rather than the material from which it is made. This encourages functional craftsmanship while allowing a wide variety of historically inspired armor designs.<br><br>

each armor type will provide a number of damage to its HP before being destroyed.<br>
To make things simple. Any hit on any armor counts for the entirety of the armor's health.

<div class='classTable'>
##### Armor Classifications
| Armor Type | Armor Hit Points | 
|:---:|:---:|
| Light | 2 | 
| Medium | 4 |
| Heavy | 6 | 

</div>

Armor type is determined by the lowest qualifying armor piece you have equipped.<br><br>

If any equipped armor piece falls into a lower armor class, your entire suit is considered that armor class.
<div class='descriptive'>
A player wearing a 15 lb chest piece, but 2 lb arms, is considered to be wearing **Light Armor**.
</div>

## Light Armor
Light Armor is armor that does not meet the minimum weight requirements for Medium Armor.

<div class='classTable'>
##### Light Armor Weight Requirements
| Armor Piece |Requirement | 
|:---:|:---:|
| Chest | 0-14 lb | 
| Arms | 0-2 lb |
| Helmet | 0-1 lb | 
| Legs | 0-4 lb | 

</div>

# Other General Rules
Falatur is designed to be easy to learn, inexpensive to play, and difficult to master. <br>

These rules will do their best to adhere to that philosphy. <br><br>

## Judges Discretion
The Judge has final authority over all safety decisions. Any equipment or action deemed unsafe may be prohibited, even if it technically complies with the written rules.<br> <br>

## Participation
Players participate at their own risk.<br>

Foam combat is a physical activity involving running, falling, and controlled weapon strikes. Minor injuries such as bruises, scrapes, and muscle soreness should be expected. <br><br>

## Safe Equipment
All weapons, shields, and equipment must pass inspection before use. <br><br>

Construction requirements are described in Weapon Construction. <br><br>

Equipment that becomes unsafe during play must be removed from the field immediately.<br><br>

A Judge may inspect equipment at any time.<br><br>

Weapons may not be intentionally thrown unless they are specifically designed and approved as thrown weapons.<br><br>

Any significant modification to approved equipment requires reinspection before use.<br><br>

## Controlled Force
Players must always fight with controlled force.<br><br>

Excessive force, reckless behavior, or intentionally attempting to injure another player is prohibited.

\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

## Blind Attacks

A player may not intentionally strike a target they cannot reasonably see.<br>
A player's vision may not be intentionally obstructed by equipment.<br><br>

## Physical Contact
Intentional punching, kicking, elbowing, kneeing, grappling, tackling, or body-checking is prohibited unless explicitly allowed by the scenario. <br>

Incidental contact during normal combat is expected.<br><br>

## Unsafe Conditions
If a player believes an unsafe condition exists, they should immediately call **"Hold!"**<br>

All players must stop fighting immediately until a Judge declares the field safe.<br><br>

## Respect The Field
Players must be aware of hazards such as holes, uneven ground, rocks, low branches, spectators, and wildlife.<br>

The Judge may modify or stop play if the battlefield becomes unsafe.<br><br>

## Modifications
No equipment may be intentionally designed to exploit ambiguities or loopholes in these rules.<br>

The Judge's decision is final.<br><br>

## Simulation
Weapons should reasonably resemble the weapon they are intended to represent in both appearance and function.<br>

Equipment intentionally designed to confuse players or circumvent the rules is prohibited.<br><br>

This is not to be confused with unique weapons. <br>

Weapons must clearly fit within one of Falatur's weapon families. If not. A Judge will determine the weapon classification of any equipment whose function is unclear. 

\page
<div class='pageNumber auto'></div>
<div class='wide'></div>

## Clothing
Players are encouraged to wear fantasy or historical clothing, but no costume is required unless specified by the event organizer.<br><br>

Clothing must allow safe movement and may not present a hazard to the player or others.<br><br>


## Sportsmanship
As outlined in the Rule of Honor:<br>
Players are expected to prioritize the safety of themselves and others above winning.<br><br>

If a situation feels unsafe, stop and ask a Judge.<br><br>

Due to this. No rule, objective, or victory is more important to a players safety.
